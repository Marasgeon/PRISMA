- Abstract—This article presents a control method for stabilizing
- a 2-DoF robot manipulator by combining Takagi-Sugeno (T-S)
- fuzzy control with a descriptor model and a high gain observer.
- The T-S fuzzy control framework is utilized to handle the
- system’s nonlinearities, employing a set of linguistic rules and
- fuzzy membership functions to capture complex relationships and
- uncertainties. Additionally, a high gain observer is integrated
- into the control system to estimate the robot’s arm velocities
- accurately. The control approach incorporates Linear Matrix
- Inequality (LMI) and Parallel Distributed Compensation (PDC)
- techniques to ensure stability and performance guarantees. The
- proposed control system’s effectiveness is demonstrated through
- simulation results in achieving stable control of the 2-DoF
- robot. The findings highlight the potential of the combined TS fuzzy control and high gain observer for enhancing the system
